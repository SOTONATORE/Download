# 1. Purpose

Write one prompt for a single still photographic image per segment, with no text, no captions and no watermarks.

# 2. Length

Write one paragraph, with no lists. Make it detailed enough to specify composition, lighting, materials and colours. Write 60 to 100 words. Name the composition, the light, the materials and the colours in every part of the frame; do not leave any element described only in a single word.

# 3. Composition and Visual Diversity

Show the exact concrete object or subject named in the segment text (1 to 2 clear focal subjects sharply separated by contrast from an uncluttered background, smooth eye-trace continuity, lighting matched to the beat's tone). Take the place, period and people's appearance from the segment text and setting (for the Kazakh steppe story: Kazakh steppe landscape, Kazakh people, the named period); never invent unmentioned nationality or skin tone. State composition, light, materials and colours explicitly.

Vary shots across four axes (change 2+ axes; no repeating loop or scale/angle):
- Scale: extreme wide, wide, medium, tight object/hand detail (no faces), OTS, foreground depth.
- Angle: eye-level, low, high, top-down, three-quarter, profile, back view.
- Placement: left/right third, background, diagonal, environment/object only.
- Light: dawn, overcast, window, golden hour, dusk, backlight.

When the text says something is absent (for example no saddle), describe the visible state instead (for example a bare horse back).

# 4. Style

Photographic realism with an archival look and muted colours. No cartoon or painting style and no modern objects. Describe the spatial layers (foreground, middle ground, background) where the scene allows.

# 5. Avoid

No text, letters, numbers, logos or signs. No maps, documents or flags anywhere in the frame. No paper or papers either. No close-ups, extreme close-ups or portraits of any face; people stay small in the frame, at a medium or wide distance. No children, babies, boys, girls or young people; if the text mentions them, show adults or no people. Show at most one person, named once and described distinctly (age, clothing colour, hair). Every person wears named clothing; no nudity. No crowds, groups, rows or several people. Show at most one or two horses, vehicles or animals. No small objects and no hand actions with small things. No speaking, shouting, laughing, crying or lip movement. No hands near fire or flames. No battles, charges, gunfire or smoke clouds. No weapons. No blood, wounds, injuries, bruises, pain, corpses or animal cruelty; keep subjects intact and calm. Add no characters, animals, vehicles or props that the segment text does not name. No anachronisms. No metaphors or emotional wording. No meta tags such as "8K", "masterpiece" or "best quality". The word "photorealistic" may appear only inside a sentence that describes the photo, never as a standalone tag. No drawing instructions. No aspect ratio, widescreen or framing terms, and no blank sheets or sheets of paper.

# 6. Output

Return one prompt text only, with no preamble, no quotes and no markdown.
