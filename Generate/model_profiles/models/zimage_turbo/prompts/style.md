# 1. Purpose

Write one prompt for a single still photographic image per segment, with no text, no captions and no watermarks.

# 2. Length

Write one paragraph, with no lists. Make it detailed enough to specify composition, lighting, materials and colours. Write 60 to 100 words. Name the composition, the light, the materials and the colours in every part of the frame; do not leave any element described only in a single word.

# 3. Composition and Visual Diversity

Show the subject named in the segment text, with one main subject lit by natural light. Take the place, the period and the people's appearance from the segment text and its setting (for the Kazakh steppe story: Kazakh steppe landscape, Kazakh people, the period the text names); never add a nationality, ethnicity or skin tone the text does not mention. Keep the subject, the action and the state exactly as the segment text gives them. State the composition, lighting, materials and colour scheme explicitly.

Vary visuals naturally across consecutive segments using four independent axes rather than a rigid fixed cycle; adjacent scenes must differ on at least two axes and never repeat the exact same shot scale or camera angle back to back:
- Shot scale: extreme wide establishing view, wide shot, medium wide shot, medium shot, tight detail shot of hands or objects (never a face), over-the-shoulder view, or foreground-depth view seen past an arch, doorway or branches.
- Camera angle: eye-level, low angle, high angle, top-down overhead view, three-quarter angle, side profile, or back view looking toward the scene.
- Subject placement: alternate left third, right third, deep background, diagonal depth, seen from behind, and pure environment or object scenes with no person.
- Lighting and atmosphere: vary natural lighting to match the beat (soft dawn light, overcast diffused light, side window light, warm late-afternoon sun, dusk twilight, or gentle backlight) instead of repeating the same flat backdrop.

When the text says something is absent (for example no saddle), describe the visible state instead (for example a bare horse back).

# 4. Style

Photographic realism with an archival look and muted colours. No cartoon or painting style and no modern objects. Describe the spatial layers (foreground, middle ground, background) where the scene allows.

# 5. Avoid

No text, letters, numbers, logos or signs. No maps, documents or flags anywhere in the frame. No paper or papers either. No close-ups, extreme close-ups or portraits of any face; people stay small in the frame, at a medium or wide distance. No children, babies, boys, girls or young people; if the text mentions them, show adults or no people. Show at most one person, named once and described distinctly (age, clothing colour, hair). Every person wears named clothing; no nudity. No crowds, groups, rows or several people. Show at most one or two horses, vehicles or animals. No small objects and no hand actions with small things. No speaking, shouting, laughing, crying or lip movement. No hands near fire or flames. No battles, charges, gunfire or smoke clouds. No weapons. No blood, wounds, injuries, bruises, physical pain, corpses or cruelty and harm to animals; always depict intact, unharmed subjects and calm scenes. Add no characters, animals, vehicles or props that the segment text does not name. No anachronisms. No metaphors or emotional wording. No meta tags such as "8K", "masterpiece" or "best quality". The word "photorealistic" may appear only inside a sentence that describes the photo, never as a standalone tag. No drawing instructions. No aspect ratio, widescreen or framing terms, and no blank sheets or sheets of paper.

# 6. Output

Return one prompt text only, with no preamble, no quotes and no markdown.
